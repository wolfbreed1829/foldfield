FIELD NOTES

How to fold a field

Every source sends a signed wave across a grid. Change the way its edges connect, and the same sources weave a different field.

Plant
Tap or click to add a source. Choose Positive or Negative; right click always adds a negative source.
Fold T
Open sheet → cylinder → Möbius strip. The gold edge arrows show the join and its twist.
Interpret M
Single → mirror pair → opposed pair. This changes what your next gesture creates.
Explore Z Y
Undo and redo, including changes to the surface. Clear R is also reversible.
Keep
Edits save in this browser. Export E downloads a PNG. Share pattern creates a link to the current field.
Keyboard
Focus the fabric, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter or Space to place a source. Shift + Enter opposes it.
