# Concept

Foldfield asks: what changes when a wave's world is sewn differently?

A source actor explores a 64 by 48 graph. The surface can have two free horizontal edges, a joined seam, or a seam that reverses vertical position. Uiua converts the shortest-path distances into signed, attenuated waves and superposes them. A native wire fabric exposes the scalar result as height and color.

Koka interprets one placement as one continuation or two. The paired continuation places the source at the antipodal grid position, preserving or reversing polarity. The surface and the meaning of a gesture are independent choices. History preserves complete immutable models, so users can retrace both kinds of choice.

The invention is the combination and interaction, not a claim to new mathematics. Manhattan geodesics, sinusoidal superposition, and Mobius topology are established ideas.

Release scope: three surfaces, three placement rules, at most sixteen sources, 128 undo states, redo, automatic session persistence, bitmap export, and native help. No database, accounts, services, plugins, or preloaded user data.
